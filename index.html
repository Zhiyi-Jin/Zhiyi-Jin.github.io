A bit about me..

I'm currently a doctoral researcher on the ERC project "A network science approach to social cohesion in European societies" (PATCHWORK) in the COALESCE Lab at the Autonomous University of Barcelona in Spain, and a PhD candidate at ICS, Department of Sociology at the University of Groningen.

My research focuses on the study of social networks in regional contexts through advanced quantitative and computational methods. In particular, I study border acquaintanceship networks, typically composed of weak ties that facilitate access to information, resources, and opportunities, and thereby enable social mobility. I am especially interested in how the composition of these networks reflects and interacts with the economic and geographical conditions of a region, shaping patterns of social and economic inequality.
